Sorted by how real each use is rather than how loud: trading bots, agent frameworks, research tools, prediction-market agents, payment rails, social personas, and a genuinely new attack surface. The money is rotating away from 'tokenize the agent' toward 'let agents trade' — and a lot of what wears the word 'AI' is a lure, not a product.
